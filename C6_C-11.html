Robotics

Robotics is about making machines called robots that follow instructions to do tasks automatically. Robots can do simple jobs or help in places that are dangerous for people. They are used in factories, hospitals, homes, and in space.

Why Robots Matter

Robots make work safer and faster. They help with hard or boring jobs and can be very accurate. In hospitals they help doctors, and at home they can help clean or assist people.

Basic Parts of a Robot

  • Sensors — detect light, sound, motion, or obstacles
  • Controller — the robot’s brain
  • Actuators — motors that move parts
  • Power Source — batteries or other energy supply
  • Frame — the body that holds everything

How Robots Understand and React

Sensors send information to the controller. The controller runs a program and tells the motors what to do. This is how a robot senses and acts.

Types of Robots

  • Industrial — used in factories
  • Medical — help in hospitals
  • Service — help at home or offices
  • Exploration — used in space or deep sea

Controllers for Learning

  • Arduino — good for starters
  • Raspberry Pi — more powerful, runs an OS
  • Micro:bit — simple and friendly for schools

Short List of Robots

  1. Mitra (India)

    Helps in hospitals and greets visitors.

  2. ASIMO (Japan)

    Can walk and climb stairs.

  3. Spot (USA)

    Dog-like robot used for inspections.

How Robotics Helps

  • Makes work safer
  • Speeds up tasks
  • Helps learning with kits

Drawbacks

  • May reduce some jobs
  • Need power and correct programs
  • Can be costly
