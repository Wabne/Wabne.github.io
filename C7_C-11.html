Robotics

Robotics is the area of technology about making and using robots. Robots are machines that follow instructions to do tasks automatically. They can do simple repeated jobs or some very hard jobs in places that are risky for people. Robots are used in factories, hospitals, homes, and in space work.

Why Robots Matter

Robots help us by making work safer and faster. They do boring or dangerous jobs and can be very accurate. In medicine they help with careful tasks, while at home some robots help clean or assist older people.

Basic Parts of a Robot

  • Sensors — they detect light, sound, motion or obstacles
  • Controller — the robot’s brain that makes decisions
  • Actuators — motors and parts that move arms, wheels, or tools
  • Power Source — like batteries that give energy
  • Frame — the body that holds everything together

How Robots Understand and React

A robot reads data from sensors and sends that data to its controller. The controller runs a program (code) and then tells the motors or tools what to do. That is how a robot senses and reacts to the world.

if(sensorValue < 20){

  moveForward();

Types of Robots

  • Industrial Robots — used in factories for tasks like welding and assembly
  • Medical Robots — help in surgeries and hospitals
  • Service Robots — help in homes, hotels, and offices
  • Exploration Robots — used in space or deep-sea work
  • Military Robots — used for certain defense tasks

Controllers Used in Learning

  • Arduino: Good for beginners, controls lights and motors
  • Raspberry Pi: More powerful, runs a full operating system
  • Micro:bit: Small and easy to use for school projects

Top Robots (Short List)

  1. Rashmi (India)

    Hindi-speaking humanoid used in schools and events to explain topics.

  2. Mitra (India)

    Greets people in hospitals and offices and can speak several languages.

  3. ASIMO (Japan)

    Humanoid robot by Honda that can walk, run, and climb stairs.

  4. Spot (USA)

    Dog-like robot used for inspections and work in unsafe places.

How Robotics Helps Our Lives

  • Makes work safer and faster
  • Helps in hospitals and factories
  • Supports learning through simple robot kits

Drawbacks of Robots

  • Can cause fewer jobs in some areas
  • Need power and correct programming
  • Can be costly to build
